# ScammerShield AI Defense — Design Brief

## Design movement
A restrained cyber-SOC interface: dark, precise, legible, and trustworthy rather than theatrical. The interface communicates that assessments are evidence-based and uncertain.

## Core principles
Evidence before spectacle; high contrast without eye strain; information hierarchy that rewards scanning; honest wording alongside every risk state; mobile-first touch targets.

## Color philosophy
Near-black navy surfaces (`#06111d`, `#0a1a2a`) create depth. Electric cyan (`#40ddff`) signals active controls and machine-readable structure. Green is reserved for low-risk/safe states, amber for caution, and coral/pink for high-risk indicators. Glows are subtle and never used as a substitute for contrast.

## Layout paradigm
A persistent desktop rail/header becomes a compact mobile drawer. The primary dashboard uses a large scan command surface, then a 12-column card grid of evidence, library, intelligence, and history. Cards have thin borders, rounded corners, and generous breathing room.

## Signature elements
Shield mark with radar cutout, fine grid texture, small uppercase section labels, evidence chips, scored meter, and “source status” disclosures.

## Interaction philosophy
Actions feel immediate, but scan results reveal their evidence instead of pretending to know the future. Loading states use a calm scanning pulse. Reduced-motion preferences are honored.

## Typography
Use a modern sans-serif for readable UI and a monospace face for scores, labels, URLs, and technical metadata. Headings use weight and letter spacing for a display-like cyber wordmark without sacrificing accessibility.

## Brand essence and voice
ScammerShield is a calm second opinion: direct, technically credible, and protective. It says “potentially unsafe based on available indicators,” never “definitely a scam.”

## Wordmark/logo
SCAMMERSHIELD with a compact AI DEFENSE badge. The shield icon uses a simple filled silhouette and negative-space radar slash so it remains legible at favicon size.

## Signature brand color
Electric cyan on deep navy, paired with a restrained coral accent for high-risk evidence.
